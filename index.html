Caravan connects busy people and small businesses with trusted human assistants who handle the everyday work — inbox, research, scheduling, data entry — at a fair price for both sides.

How Caravan works

Both sides sign up, say what they need or offer, and we match them on skills, language and price. Money sits in escrow until the work is approved.

1

Create a profile

Clients describe the work and budget. Agents list their skills, languages and hourly rate.

2

Post and match

Post a task and see ranked matches, or browse the directory and hire someone directly.

3

Fund escrow

Pay into escrow by card, PayPal, bank, local wallet or crypto. The agent can see the work is funded.

4

Approve hours

Agents log hours. Each approval releases pay; unused funds come back to you.
